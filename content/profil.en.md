<p style="hyphens: auto;">Dr. phil. Monika Kast Perry<br/>
I am holding the titles “Fachpsychologin für Kinder- und Jugendpsychologie FSP” and “eidgenössisch anerkannte Psychotherapeutin”.
</p>
<p style="hyphens: auto;">I completed the teacher training college in 1998 and studied psychology at the University of Zurich from 1999 to 2005, specializing in neuropsychology. I worked as an elementary school teacher in various grades before and during my studies.
</p>

I accomplished my PhD thesis on dyslexia at the Institute of Neuropsychology at the University of Zurich in 2010. Subsequently, I conducted two post-doctoral fellowships in Switzerland and California, USA. Since 2012 I have been working as a school psychologist. Currently I am helding an 80% position with the school psychological service for the secondary school in Regensdorf / Buchs / Dällikon since March 2018.

I completed my therapeutic training, based on a systemic approach at the IEF in Zurich, in June 2017. I have been running my own private practice since March 2018, and since August 2021 in Birr. I have participated in further training at the EMDR Institute Switzerland, the Institute for Schema Therapy Ostschweiz Winterthur, as well as hypnosystemic training programs at the IEF in Zurich.
