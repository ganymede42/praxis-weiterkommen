## Dr. phil. Monika Kast Perry

I hold the titles “Fachpsychologin für Kinder- und Jugendpsychologie FSP” and “eidgenössisch anerkannte Psychotherapeutin”.

## Education and professional experience

I completed teacher training in 1998 and studied psychology at the University of Zurich from 1999 to 2005, specialising in neuropsychology. Before and during my studies, I worked as an elementary school teacher across different age groups and levels.

I completed my doctoral dissertation on dyslexia at the Institute of Neuropsychology at the University of Zurich in 2010. Subsequently, I held postdoctoral positions in Switzerland and abroad. I have worked as a school psychologist since 2012 and have held an 80% position with the school psychological service for the secondary school in Regensdorf / Buchs / Dällikon since March 2018.

## Therapeutic training and continuing education

I completed my therapeutic training, based on a systemic approach, at the IEF in Zurich in June 2017. I have been working in private practice since March 2018 and in my practice in Birr since August 2021. I have completed further training at the EMDR Institute Switzerland, the Institute for Schema Therapy Ostschweiz Winterthur, and hypnosystemic training programmes at the IEF in Zurich.
