Therapy for adolescents, families and adults

Counseling in crisis situations

Monika Kast Perry

Praxis weiterkommen offers:

Psychological support for both children and adolescents

and their parents regarding emotional and social problems occurring in school and family.
     The objective is to find creative and practical solutions for complex situations. To achieve this goal, a close collaboration between family members, therapist, and other professionals and
    educators is often required.

Psychotherapy for adults

aims to strengthen their resources and finding appropriate solutions for conflicts and crisis
    situations.

Counseling and coaching for teachers

and teaching teams for school related questions and conflicts. The resources and solutions oriented approach
    will be applied.
